# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1: Core Construction Management
- Authentication, users, roles, project management
- Supervisor assignment and automated notification
- Worker management and rate history
- Attendance and working hours
- Automatic labour calculation
- Labour approval and audit trail
- Daily reports and report approval
- Materials and inventory tracking
- Photos, issues, progress, site diary
- Admin dashboard and PDF-ready exports

## Phase 2: Advanced Construction Management
- Equipment, maintenance, and fuel management
- Suppliers, procurement, purchase orders, and delivery notes
- Expenses, budgets, labour budget monitoring, and forecasting
- BOQ and variation/change order management
- Safety inspections, corrective actions, document management
- Scheduling, milestones, subcontractors, and client management

## Phase 3: Automation and Expansion
- WhatsApp and SMS notifications
- Offline synchronization and mobile-first enhancements
- Client portal and automated progress reports
- Advanced analytics, forecasting, and AI-assisted summaries
- Dedicated mobile application and field verification workflows
